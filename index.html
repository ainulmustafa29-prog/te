    Keep Your Real Email Private

    Disposable email helps you avoid exposing your primary email address when signing up for services, downloading content, or testing products. Using a temporary address reduces the chance that your personal mailbox receives unwanted or unsolicited mail.

    Be aware: temporary email services are not a guarantee of 100% anonymity or complete privacy. Emails are still transmitted over the internet, and mail providers may log traffic. Do not use a temporary mailbox for anything sensitive or confidential.
